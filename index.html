Attendance & Internal Mark

1. Attendance Point Hmuh Dan

  • Present: Ni pangngaiah point 1 (Inrinniah point 5).
  • Reported: Ni pangngaiah point 0.5 (Inrinniah point 2.5).
  • Absent: Point 0.

2. Chawlh In-Report Dan

Class i kal theih dawn loh chuan Menu aṭangin "📝 Chawlh Dilna & In-Report-na" ah lut la, i chawlh chhan ziakin thehlut rawh. Zirtirtuin a pawm (approve) hunah i attendance kha 'Reported' ah a inthlak ang a, point chanve i hmu ang.

3. Internal Mark Hamthatna

Class i kal ṭhat dan (percentage) hian Internal Mark-ah nghawng thui tak a nei a ni. Entirnan, DCA 104 subject-ah phei chuan i Attendance Percentage aṭangin Practical Exam Mark (Max 40) pek auto i ni dawn a ni. Class i kal that poh leh i exam mark a tha dawn tihna a ni.
